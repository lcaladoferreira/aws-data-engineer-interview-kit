# Job-ready Career Guides

Practical, claim-safe guidance.

<!-- item -->
## 1. LinkedIn profile guide

Headline = role + platform + outcome; About = proof-led narrative; Featured = two strongest repositories; Experience = measurable bullets; Skills = role-specific; publish architecture lessons without exposing employer data.

<!-- item -->
## 2. Naukri profile guide

Complete every searchable field truthfully, align title and skills with target roles, keep notice period and location accurate, refresh after material changes, and never promise a fixed number of interview calls.

<!-- item -->
## 3. Behavioral interview guide

Prepare STAR stories for incidents, disagreement, ambiguity, cost, quality, delivery and failure. Separate your personal action from the team's result.

<!-- item -->
## 4. Product sense interview guide

Clarify user and decision, define north-star and guardrail metrics, model data grain and latency, surface privacy and cost, then propose an experiment.

<!-- item -->
## 5. Referral templates

Short template: “Hi [Name], I’m applying for [role]. My relevant proof is [one concrete system/result]. If you’re comfortable referring me, here is the vacancy and resume. No worries if not.”

<!-- item -->
## 6. AWS Data Engineer roadmap

Follow the official exam guide, Skill Builder plan, hands-on labs, Well-Architected guidance, then create fresh-clone-verifiable projects.
